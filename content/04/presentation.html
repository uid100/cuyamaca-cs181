Limited Extra Credit Opportunity!

Looking for volunteers!

Students may work individually or in groups of 2 or 3 students (maximum of 3 students per group) to prepare and deliver a short lesson on loops using Chapter 5 as your guide.

Presentation Topic

Your lesson should explain the following concepts:

  • What are for loops and while loops?
  • How do they work?
  • How are they similar?
  • How are they different?
  • Why would a programmer choose one instead of the other?
  • What is a loop control variable?

General Instructions

  • Prepare a presentation to support your lesson.
  • Google Slides is recommended because it makes collaboration easier.
  • Microsoft PowerPoint is also an acceptable option.
  • If working with a partner or group, all students must participate in the presentation and discussion.

Presentation Requirements

  • Present your lesson content for at least 5 minutes.
  • Lead a question-and-answer discussion for the remaining time, for a total presentation length of approximately 10 minutes.
  • Questions may come from classmates and/or the instructor(s).

Possible Extra Credit

This is an optional assignment, but it may be worth up to 100 points, equivalent to the value of a lab exercise.

Take advantage of this opportunity to strengthen your understanding of loops while earning extra credit!
